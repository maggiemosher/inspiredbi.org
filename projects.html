E2A: Evidence-to-Action AI Enhanced Learning Solutions

A unified, multi-agent system that connects school data, Ci3T research evidence, and implementation planning. Four specialized agents work under a governance layer, the Grounded Plane, that keeps every output grounded, private, and human-approved.

  • Diagnostic Agent: overnight data review
  • Synthesis Agent: evidence + local curriculum
  • Planning Agent: implementation blueprints
  • Evaluation Agent: fidelity, outcomes, attendance
