Agentic AI: A Sequenced Learning Path for Leaders

Three self-paced Coursera offerings, ordered from conceptual overview to hands-on building. Start at the top and work down — each one assumes the vocabulary of the one before it.

  1. Agentic AI and AI Agents: A Primer for Leaders

    • Vanderbilt University
    • Beginner
    • ~6 hours
    • 4.7 ★

    The strongest starting point. Establishes what an agent actually is, how tool use works, and where autonomy belongs in an organization — framed for decision-makers rather than developers.

  2. Generative and Agentic AI

    • Saïd Business School, University of Oxford
    • Beginner
    • ~10 hours
    • 4.8 ★

    One level deeper on the mechanics: large language models, prompting technique, and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG). This is the bridge between understanding agents and building them. Note that it runs in scheduled cohorts rather than fully on demand.

  3. Microsoft Generative and Agentic AI Basics

    • Microsoft
    • 4-course specialization
    • Intermediate
    • ~64 hours
    • 3.7 ★

    The build path. Most directly aligned with the Microsoft 365 and Azure ecosystem, including Azure AI Foundry and end-to-end agent development. Budget roughly two months at eight hours per week.

Shortest useful route: items 1 and 2 together run about sixteen hours and are enough to lead an informed conversation about agentic AI. Add item 3 only when the goal shifts from evaluating agents to building them inside Microsoft tooling.
