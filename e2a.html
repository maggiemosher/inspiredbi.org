E2A: Evidence-to-Action AI Enhanced Learning Solutions

A unified, multi-agent system. Instead of one large AI model, E2A hands complex school-improvement work to four specialized agents that work together. A foundation layer, the Grounded Plane, keeps every agent accurate, safe, and aligned with district goals.

Open the leadership dashboard → Take the 10-minute reviewer tour

The problem

Data is available. Action is hard.

Schools collect screening, fidelity, attendance, and climate data, and many platforms display it. Leadership teams still lack help to interpret it, choose a response, and coordinate the work. Project ENHANCE (R324N190002) documented these needs across Ci3T schools.

The approach

One connected cycle, not more tools

E2A links data, research evidence, the district’s own curriculum, and implementation supports in one process: identify → connect → plan → monitor. It does not add another standalone dashboard or resource library.

The guarantee

People decide. AI supports.

Every AI statement is traced to verified data or research. Student data stays inside privacy boundaries. Leadership teams approve every hand-off. E2A never makes an educational decision on its own.

A day with E2A

How the cycle runs at Roosevelt Elementary (simulated)

  1. 11:00 PM – 5:45 AM
    Diagnostic Agent integrates the day’s data and prepares the morning brief.
    No staff time needed. Runs inside the district’s secure environment.
  2. 7:15 AM
    Principal reviews 3 flagged needs in 10 minutes and confirms 2 for team follow-up.
    Human checkpoint 1 · logged by the Grounded Plane
  3. 7:16 AM
    Synthesis Agent retrieves Ci3T modules, practice guides, and the Grade 4 SEL unit being taught now.
    Every statement linked to its source
  4. 2:45 PM (team meeting)
    Planning Agent co-pilots the leadership team through strategy choice and drafts the plan with owners, timeline, and PL needs.
    Human checkpoint 2 · team approves the plan
  5. Every day after
    Evaluation Agent tracks fidelity, outcomes, and attendance; it warns the team early when attendance slips.
    Human checkpoint 3 · team approves adjustments

Who does what

E2A divides the work so educators spend their time on judgment, not data wrangling.

Division of work between AI agents and educators

        Foundation · Governance and orchestration

        The Grounded Plane

        The permanent oversight layer under all four agents. It enforces privacy boundaries, requires every AI output to rest on verified research and district data, keeps people in charge of decisions, and manages every hand-off so the agents work as one coordinated system.

          Accessibility & data statement

          • Designed to meet WCAG 2.2 AA: skip link, labeled landmarks, full keyboard operation, visible focus, and text contrast of at least 4.5:1.
          • Meaning is never shown by color alone. Status uses icons and words; chart series are labeled; every chart has a data table.
          • Display options: dark mode, high contrast, reduced motion, and larger text. Settings carry across pages.
          • Works on phones and tablets; prints cleanly (use “Print / save as PDF” in the Planning Agent).
          • Data: Roosevelt Elementary, Prairie Valley USD, all classrooms, and all numbers are simulated for illustration. Ci3T measure and module names link to the real, openly available resources at ci3t.org.
          • AI: Agent outputs on this page are scripted examples of intended behavior. They are not live model output.
